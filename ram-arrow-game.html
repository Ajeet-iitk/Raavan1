Yuddha Kanda — The Ten Heads of Raavan

Raavan has ten heads. A single arrow cannot fell him — every head must be struck and burned away, while he mocks you from the walls of Lanka.

Aim at the heads. Hold to draw — a dotted trail shows exactly where the arrow will fly, and your last two shots stay marked in the air. Release to loose.

Raavan fights back! He looses arrows — duck beneath them — and hurls bombs — leap over the blast. Take a hit and you lose an arrow.

Twelve arrows. Ten heads. The vanara army of Sugriva roars at your back; Raavan's asuras cower behind him.
