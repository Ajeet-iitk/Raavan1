Yuddha Kanda — The Ten Heads of Raavan

Raavan has ten heads. A single arrow cannot fell him — every head must be struck and burned away. He will mock you while you draw.

Aim at the heads. Hold to draw the bow, release to loose the arrow. Wind drifts your shot — watch the banner and the aim trail.

Twelve arrows. Ten heads. When the last head falls, Lanka burns and Sita is free.
